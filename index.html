Overview

MOTIF is a large-scale human motion capture dataset comprising approximately 65 hours of motion data, captured using 40 Vicon Vero 2.2 cameras. It features expressive whole-body movements with detailed finger articulations, paired with natural language descriptions. The dataset includes numerous long-horizon motion sequences exceeding 30 seconds, spanning a wide range of human behaviors.

MOTIF covers diverse motion categories, including locomotion, dance, object interaction, scene interaction, cinematic acting, and everyday activities. The motions range from expressive walking and running variations to sitting, lying down, and other natural daily behaviors.

Motion Diversity

(a) Locomotion

MOTIF includes diverse locomotion patterns beyond conventional walking and running. Examples include running while flapping the arms like wings, walking while clutching the chest, walking with outstretched arms, crouched walking, jumping, and other expressive locomotion variations.

(b) Dance Movement

MOTIF contains a wide variety of dance movements, featuring coordinated whole-body dynamics, expressive arm movements, and natural finger gestures.

(c) Object Interaction

MOTIF includes motions involving interactions with everyday objects, capturing coordinated body movements and natural hand and finger gestures.
